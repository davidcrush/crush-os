# Code of Conduct

Crush OS is a learning project and a safe space. Every person is welcome here.

David Crush is neurodivergent (Autism and ADHD). This project exists in part because the world is often not designed for people like him. We will not recreate that here.

## Who is welcome

Everyone. Identity is not up for debate. That includes, without limitation, race, ethnicity, nationality, sex, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, neurotype, religion, age, class, and body.

You do not have to earn belonging. You do not have to mask to participate. You do have to treat other people as people.

## What we do not tolerate

We refuse **behavior**, not identity. The following are not welcome:

- Hate speech, slurs, or “jokes” that punch down
- Racism, sexism, homophobia, transphobia, ableism, or other prejudice
- Harassment, threats, stalking, or doxxing
- Sexual content involving minors
- Persistent bad-faith arguing after a boundary has been stated
- Using the project to harm people

Intent is not a free pass. If the effect is abuse or exclusion, stop.

Disagreement about code, design, or docs is expected. Personal attacks are not disagreement.

## How we communicate

These norms are for humans and for AI agents working in this repo.

- **Be explicit.** Say what you mean. Do not rely on sarcasm, subtext, or “you know what I meant.”
- **Use plain language.** Define jargon the first time it matters. A glossary lives in [docs/os-primer.md](docs/os-primer.md).
- **Prefer writing.** Decisions belong in docs and ADRs, not only in chat.
- **No sarcasm as the default.** Dry humor is fine when it is clearly humor. Mockery is not.
- **Assume competence and ask anyway.** “Did you mean X?” is better than guessing.
- **Correct kindly.** Point at the work, not the person.
- **Access matters.** Prefer high-contrast, predictable structure, and no flashing or seizure-risk gimmicks if we ever add visuals.

## Enforcement

This is a solo-maintainer project. David Crush decides what violates this code.

- A first, unclear misstep may get a direct correction.
- Clear or repeated harm gets a removal from the conversation, the repo, or both.
- There is no debate over whether people deserve to exist here.

If you are unsure whether something is okay, do not post it. Ask, or leave it out.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any Crush OS space that points here. It also applies to AI-generated text committed to this project. Agents must follow it.

The spirit of this document is closer to a community safety agreement than to a corporate policy. If you need a longer general template, the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) is a common starting point. This file is the one that applies here.
